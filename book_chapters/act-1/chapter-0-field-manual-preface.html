Act I: Foundations of Breach

Chapter 0: The Dark Side of BPF

I cracked open the field manual in a windowless office lit by the glow of network graphs. They sold eBPF as a monitoring interface—debug tools and harmless tracers. I saw something else: the skeleton key to the kernel’s front door.

Picture this: a patchpoint at every syscall, a hook on every security decision. All courtesy of BPF bytecode running in ring-0. No zero-day needed—just the kernel’s own API turned against itself.

The manual you hold isn’t a bug report or a CVE write-up. It’s a manifesto. A confession that the most elegant kernel feature ever built can be weaponized with surgical precision. Every bypass you’ll read is a feature, not a flaw.

In Act I, we dismantle the myth of “safe” observability. You’ll learn how eBPF outmaneuvers seccomp, outsmarts capabilities, and whispers lies to SELinux. We’ll lay the groundwork for turning official kernel hooks into backdoors that vanish from every log.

I’ve spent years riding the edge between red teams and purple teams. I’ve seen defenders praise eBPF for real-time tracing while blind to its true potential: rewriting the kernel’s story mid-execution. This chapter sets the stage.

Brace yourself: we’re about to reshape trust at the lowest level. By the end of Act I, “mandatory” controls will feel like polite suggestions. And you’ll know why every security boundary you thought was sacred can be bent with a few lines of code.

Welcome to the dark side of BPF. The real journey begins now.
